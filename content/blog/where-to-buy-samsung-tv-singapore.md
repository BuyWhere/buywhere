## How to choose

- **Need the lowest price on 55\" or below?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Buying 65\" or larger?** Brick-and-mortar retailers (Harvey Norman, Courts, Best Denki, Samsung Experience Store SG) bundle free delivery, wall-mounting, and old-TV disposal — usually worth the SGD 50–200 premium over the marketplace price.
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment?** Harvey Norman, Courts, Best Denki, and Challenger all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi). The headline price is official retail.

**Are Samsung TV prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Samsung Experience Store SG, Harvey Norman, Courts, Best Denki, and Challenger.
