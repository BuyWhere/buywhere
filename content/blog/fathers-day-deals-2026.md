## When Is Father's Day 2026?

Father's Day 2026 falls on **Sunday, June 21** in the United States, Canada, and most countries. In the UK, Ireland, and Australia, it's celebrated on the third Sunday of June — which is **June 21** this year too. That means the biggest sales hit the week leading up to June 19–21.
