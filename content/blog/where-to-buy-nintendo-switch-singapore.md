## OLED vs Lite vs original Switch

Choose **Nintendo Switch OLED** for most buyers. The OLED screen is better for handheld play, the kickstand is stronger, and it works with the TV dock. Choose **Switch Lite** if the console is only for handheld play, especially for kids or travel. Avoid paying close-to-OLED prices for the older LCD model unless it comes in a genuinely strong bundle.

If a successor console is influencing your timing, buy only when you need the library now or when the discount is meaningful. Nintendo's first-party games hold value, so bundles with games you actually want can be better than a small console discount.

## How to judge bundle value

A bundle is only cheaper if the extras are useful:

- First-party games you would buy separately.
- An extra pair of Joy-Con controllers for family play.
- A carrying case for travel.
- A microSD card from a reputable brand.
- A charging dock or grip from a known accessory maker.

Ignore generic screen protectors, no-name accessories, or forced game choices when calculating value.
