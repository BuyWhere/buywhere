## How to pick the right channel

- **Need the lowest price and willing to time a sale?** Watch Shopee and Lazada platform events. Stacking Shopee free-shipping vouchers and credit-card rebates can shave another 5–8%.
- **Want guaranteed Apple SG warranty + trade-in?** Apple Store SG is the canonical channel. Trade-in credit on older iPhones can drop effective price by SGD 200–500 depending on model.
- **Need to spread the cost over 24 months?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman offer 0% instalment plans with DBS, UOB, OCBC, and Citibank cards.
- **Buying the 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max?** Stock is tighter outside Apple Store SG; check multiple retailers if you need a specific colour/storage combo.
