**Quick Answer:** For the **lowest price** on AirPods 4 or Pro 2, Shopee SG and Lazada SG run SGD 10–30 below official retail during platform sales. ## How to choose

- **Need the lowest price?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Need engraving on AirPods Max?** Apple Store SG online orders can engrave AirPods Max for free (1–3 business days).
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi). The headline price is official retail.
- **Buying AirPods Pro 2 with USB-C?** Apple Store SG, iStudio, and Shopee Mall all carry the USB-C variant. Lightning-only AirPods 2/3 are usually clearance-priced at authorised resellers.

## Common questions

**Are AirPods prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, and Best Denki. Shopee Mall and LazMall Apple-authorised sellers can undercut by SGD 10–30 during platform sales.

**Where do I buy AirPods Max in Singapore?** AirPods Max is widely available at Apple Store SG, iStudio, Challenger, Harvey Norman, and Shopee Mall. Engraving is only available through Apple Store SG online.

**Is AppleCare+ worth it for AirPods?** AppleCare+ for Headphones covers 2 incidents of accidental damage per 24 months. For AirPods Max (which retails in the four-figure SGD range), it's typically worth it. For AirPods 4 or Pro 2, the per-incident fee is usually close to the replacement cost.
