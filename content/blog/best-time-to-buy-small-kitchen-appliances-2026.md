## Hidden cost: extended warranty

Most kitchen appliances come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Extended warranties through retailers (Best Buy Total, Target Protection, Asurion) cost $30–$80 per item and only pay out in specific failure modes.

For **premium appliances** ($500+): buy the manufacturer warranty if you can. Vitamix, Breville, and De'Longhi warranties through the manufacturer are usually 2–3 years for the same price.

For **mid-range** ($150–$400): skip the warranty. Most failures happen in the first 90 days (return policy covers that), and the failure rate after year 1 is low.

For **budget** (under $150): definitely skip the warranty. The warranty costs half the product price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is it worth buying a refurbished kitchen appliance in 2026?**
A: For Vitamix, Breville, and De'Longhi, yes — manufacturer refurbs have the same warranty as new units and run 15–25% off. For Ninja, Instant Pot, and Cuisinart, the refurb discount is smaller and the new-product warranty is comparable, so new is often better.

**Q: Are kitchen appliance Black Friday deals actually better than Prime Day?**
A: For Nespresso, Cuisinart, and Instant Pot, yes — Black Friday is 5–10% deeper. For Ninja and Vitamix, Black Friday is dramatically deeper (30% off vs 10% Prime Day). For Breville and De'Longhi, the two are roughly tied. BuyWhere MCP tracks year-round and `notifyOnDrop` catches both.

**Q: Should I buy a kitchen appliance as a gift now or wait for the recipient's birthday?**
A: Buy now only if there's a strong sale (Prime Day carryover July 25–Aug 10 still has Nespresso at $89). Otherwise wait for Black Friday and gift the appliance + receipt + extended return window. Most retailers (Best Buy, Macy's, Williams Sonoma) extend the holiday return window through January 31.
