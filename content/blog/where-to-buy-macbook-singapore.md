# Where to Buy MacBook in Singapore (2026) — Every Merchant Compared

Singapore buyers can choose from a dozen reliable channels when shopping for MacBook, each with different strengths in price, warranty, and instalment flexibility. This guide maps every major Singapore MacBook merchant, what they stock, and how to pick the right one.

**Quick Answer:** For the **lowest price** on MacBook Air M-series, Shopee SG and Lazada SG run SGD 100–250 below official retail during platform sales. For **student/Edu pricing (5–10% off)** and AppleCare+, Apple Store SG (Edu + retail) is canonical. For **24-month 0% instalment**, Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman dominate.

## How to choose

- **Need the lowest price?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Are you a student or educator?** Apple Store for Education offers 5–10% off MacBook Air and Pro with a valid student email. Apple Store SG staff can also process Edu pricing in-store with the right documents.
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi). The headline price is official retail.
- **Need a specific configuration?** Apple Store SG has the full configurator (RAM, SSD, chip). Shopee/Lazada sellers typically stock only the base configs and 1–2 popular middle SKUs.

## Common questions

**Are MacBook prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, and Best Denki. Shopee Mall and LazMall Apple-authorised sellers can undercut by SGD 100–250 during platform sales.

**Where do I buy MacBook Pro M4 in Singapore?** MacBook Pro M4 is currently available at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Harvey Norman, Shopee Mall, and LazMall. For higher-spec configurations, Apple Store SG is the most reliable source.

**Is the AppleCare+ bundle worth it for MacBook in Singapore?** AppleCare+ for MacBook covers 2 incidents of accidental damage per 24 months and extends phone support to 90 days. For MacBook Pro M-series, the per-incident fee is typically much lower than out-of-warranty repair, so it's usually worth it.
