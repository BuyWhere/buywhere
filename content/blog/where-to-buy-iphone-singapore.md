# Where to Buy iPhone in Singapore (2026) — Every Merchant Compared

Singapore buyers can choose from two dozen reliable channels when shopping for any iPhone model — Apple authorised retailers plus all three local telcos (Singtel, StarHub, M1). This guide maps every major Singapore iPhone merchant, what they stock, and how to pick the right one.

**Quick Answer:** For the **lowest outright price** on iPhone 16 / 15, Shopee SG and Lazada SG run SGD 80–250 below official retail during platform sales. For **24-month 0% instalment with data**, Singtel, StarHub, and M1 dominate. For **Edu pricing and same-day collection**, Apple Store SG is canonical.

## How to choose

- **Need the lowest outright price?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Already on a Singtel/StarHub/M1 plan that's up for renewal?** A telco contract at SGD 0–100 upfront plus 24 months of instalment is usually the cheapest overall path. Compare the bundle (data + device) against the outright price + SIM-only plan.
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment without a contract?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi). The headline price is official retail.
- **Switching from iPhone 14 or older?** Apple Store SG, iStudio, and most merchants offer trade-in credits that can offset SGD 200–800 of the new device price.

## Common questions

**Are iPhone prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, and Best Denki. Shopee Mall and LazMall Apple-authorised sellers can undercut by SGD 80–250 during platform sales. Telco contract pricing depends on the chosen data plan.

**iPhone 16, 15, or 14 — which is the best value in Singapore?** iPhone 16 is the current flagship with Apple Intelligence support. iPhone 15 is the strong value pick — same general form factor, similar cameras, but no Camera Control hardware. iPhone 14 is the budget pick; lacks Apple Intelligence and the Dynamic Island.

**Where do I buy iPhone SE in Singapore?** iPhone SE (3rd gen) is still available at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Harvey Norman, and most authorised resellers. It's the most affordable iPhone in Singapore that supports current iOS.
