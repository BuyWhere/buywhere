# Where to Buy iPhone 16 in Singapore (2026) — Retailer and Deal Guide

Then compare Shopee Mall, LazMall, Amazon SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, iStudio, and the three major telcos. ## Which iPhone 16 storage tier should you buy?

For most Singapore buyers, **256GB is the safest iPhone 16 tier**. The 128GB model is fine for light users who stream most media and back up photos aggressively, but it can feel tight if you shoot a lot of video. The 512GB tier is for creators, families recording 4K clips, or people who keep years of photos offline.

Do not overpay for storage inside a weak bundle. If a merchant bundles a 512GB phone with accessories you do not need, compare it against the exact 256GB or 512GB SIM-free model before deciding.

## SIM-free vs telco contract

A telco deal can look cheap because the phone subsidy is spread across monthly payments. Compare the **full contract cost**:

1. Upfront device payment.
2. Monthly plan cost above your current plan.
3. Contract length.
4. Trade-in credit and conditions.
5. Roaming, data, and family-line discounts you will actually use.

If the plan is more expensive than your current plan, the subsidy may disappear over 24 months.

## Common questions

**Where is the safest place to buy iPhone 16 in Singapore?**

Apple Store Singapore is the clearest warranty path. Authorised resellers, telcos, Shopee Mall, LazMall, and Amazon SG are also reasonable when the listing clearly states local Apple warranty.

**Is iPhone 16 still worth buying in 2026?**

Yes when the discount versus iPhone 17 is meaningful. If the price gap is small for your chosen storage tier, compare the newer model before buying.

**Should I buy used or refurbished iPhone 16?**

Used can be good value, but confirm battery health, warranty status, iCloud lock status, and return terms. For lower friction, choose Apple or authorised refurbished channels when available.
