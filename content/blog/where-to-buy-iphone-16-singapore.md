# Where to Buy an iPhone 16 in Singapore in 2026

Singapore is one of Apple's most concentrated retail markets in Southeast Asia, and the iPhone 16 family — base, Plus, Pro, Pro Max, and the cheaper iPhone 16e — is sold through a tight network of Apple Premium Resellers (APRs), the Apple Online Store, the three incumbent telcos (Singtel, StarHub, M1), and the MVNO Circles.Life. The decision that matters most is not "who has the lowest headline price today" but "SIM-free or contract-locked, and which carrier's plan am I going to use for the next 24 months?" This guide works through that decision, the warranty implications of each channel, and the three annual sale windows that produce the lowest SG prices.

## Channels that sell iPhone 16 in Singapore

Four channels matter:

- **Apple Online Store (apple.com/sg)** — Apple's own retail. Trade-In programme, AppleCare+, the full accessory range. Rarely runs outright discounts; the price advantage is the trade-in top-up.
- **Apple Premium Resellers (APRs)** — iStudio (Robinson Road, Wisma Atrium, NEX, Bugis Junction, Tampines, Jurong Point, VivoCity, IMM, Waterway Point, Jewel Changi), Epicentre, and a few smaller authorised chains. APRs are the SG retail channel that most aggressively discounts, runs its own vouchers, and bundles accessories.
- **Telcos — Singtel, StarHub, M1, and Circles.Life** — contract-locked sales, 24-month plans, the device subsidised against the monthly contract. The headline price is the lowest but the total cost of ownership depends on the plan value.
- **Parallel importers and direct-from-overseas** — Carousell private sellers, direct Japan/HK/US imports, and one or two grey-market importers. Cheapest at headline, no Apple Singapore warranty, no AppleCare+.

For most SG buyers the choice is APR (SIM-free) vs telco (contract-locked), and the cheaper channel depends on whether you want a contract.

## SIM-free from an APR — the right answer if you want flexibility

SIM-free iPhone 16 from an APR is the right pick if:

- You want to keep your current telco or switch telcos freely over the next 24 months.
- You travel frequently and want a dual-SIM setup (one physical SIM + one eSIM) with no carrier lock to manage.
- You plan to resell on Carousell when you upgrade in 18–24 months — unlocked devices command a noticeably higher resale price.
- You are buying for a family member or business where device ownership matters.

APRs honour the same one-year Apple Singapore limited warranty as the Apple Online Store. AppleCare+ can be added at point of sale and adds two more years of coverage including two incidents of accidental damage per 12 months (excess applies). The APR will register the AppleCare+ on Apple's systems at sale.

## Contract-locked from a telco — the right answer if you need a plan anyway

Singtel, StarHub, M1, and Circles.Life all run iPhone 16 promotions on their flagship 24-month plans. The math is straightforward: the device subsidy minus the value of the plan over 24 months, net of opportunity cost on the cash you did not spend upfront.

As a rule of thumb, if you are paying SGD 80–120/month for a postpaid plan that includes generous data (50GB+), no roaming activation fee, and bundled entertainment (Netflix, Disney+, or Spotify Family), the telco iPhone 16 deal is cheaper than SIM-free over the 24-month contract term.

The trade-off is the IMEI lock: the phone is locked to the issuing telco for the full 24 months. You can request an unlock after contract end (free, takes a few working days), but if you want to travel, switch telcos, or sell the device mid-contract, you will pay an early termination fee that wipes out most of the subsidy.

## Parallel-import and overseas imports — only for specific use cases

Parallel-import (often labelled "AP set" or "Asia set") iPhones are usually 5–10% cheaper at the SIM-free price point. The trade-offs are real:

- **No Apple Singapore warranty.** Apple Singapore will refuse to service the device even out of warranty. Repairs go through the importer's own service centre, which may take weeks for parts.
- **No AppleCare+.** You cannot buy AppleCare+ for a parallel-import iPhone in Singapore.
- **Different region power supply.** US and JPN iPhones ship with the wrong power adapter for Singapore and sometimes a physically different SIM tray, though the device itself runs fine on any region 5G band.
- **Resale value is lower** because of the warranty and AppleCare gap.

This channel is reasonable if you are buying a second device, gifting to a relative overseas, or specifically want a JPN/US-region Apple ID for cheaper App Store and iCloud pricing. For a primary phone you will use for two years, the missing warranty makes parallel import a bad bet.

## Three sale windows that produce the lowest SG price

Three predictable windows consistently beat the everyday APR price:

1. **Great Singapore Sale (GSS), June–July** — APR-wide vouchers and Apple-authorised partner promos. The headline SGD savings are typically SGD 80–200 off the iPhone 16 RRP, depending on configuration.
2. **Lazada and Shopee mega sales (9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12)** — APR stores run vouchers on these platforms, and platform vouchers stack on top. The Apple Online Store itself rarely discounts here, but iStudio's Shopee store consistently does.
3. **September launch window** — when Apple announces the next-generation iPhone, APRs aggressively clear current-generation stock at real discounts. This is the cheapest single window for the existing iPhone 16 family.

Telco promotions run on their own calendar, mostly around Chinese New Year, National Day, and the September iPhone launch. Telco pricing is usually best in the September window when telcos subsidise aggressively to win 24-month contracts.

## How to choose between the four iPhone 16 SKUs

- **iPhone 16e** — the cheapest Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone, single rear camera, no ProMotion. The right pick if you want the lowest entry price and do not need the second camera or 120Hz.
- **iPhone 16 (base)** — the mainstream pick. Dual rear cameras, full MagSafe, Dynamic Island, A18 chip. The right answer for most buyers.
- **iPhone 16 Plus** — the battery-life pick. Same hardware as the base 16 with a larger 6.7-inch display and a meaningfully bigger battery.
- **iPhone 16 Pro / Pro Max** — the ProMotion 120Hz display, the A18 Pro chip, the telephoto camera, and ProMotion video features. Worth the premium if you shoot a lot of video or play games that benefit from the higher GPU throughput.
