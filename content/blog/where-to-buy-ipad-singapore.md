# Where to Buy iPad in Singapore (2026) — Every Merchant Compared

Singapore buyers can choose from a dozen reliable channels when shopping for iPad, each with different strengths in price, warranty, and instalment flexibility. This guide maps every major Singapore iPad merchant, what they stock, and how to pick the right one.

**Quick Answer:** For the **lowest price** on iPad Air M3 and iPad (10th/11th gen), Shopee SG and Lazada SG run SGD 50–150 below official retail during platform sales. For **student/Edu pricing (5–10% off)** and engraving, Apple Store SG is canonical. For **24-month 0% instalment**, Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman dominate.

## How to choose

- **Need the lowest price?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Are you a student or educator?** Apple Store for Education offers 5–10% off iPad Air and iPad Pro with a valid student email. Apple Store SG staff can also process Edu pricing in-store.
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi).
- **Need cellular (Wi-Fi + Cellular)?** Apple Store SG and iStudio handle eSIM provisioning on the spot for Singtel, StarHub, and M1.

## Common questions

**Are iPad prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, and Best Denki. Shopee Mall and LazMall Apple-authorised sellers can undercut by SGD 50–150 during platform sales.

**Where do I buy iPad Pro M4 in Singapore?** iPad Pro M4 is available at Apple Store SG, Challenger, Harvey Norman, Shopee Mall, and LazMall. For higher-spec configurations (1TB / 2TB with Nano-texture glass), Apple Store SG is the most reliable source.

**Is iPad or iPad Air better for students in Singapore?** For most students, the standard iPad (10th gen or newer) is sufficient — it has the full app ecosystem, Apple Pencil support, and starts at a lower price. iPad Air M3 is worth the upgrade only if you need the M3 chip for creative apps or want the larger 13\" screen.
