## How to choose

- **Need the lowest price on PS5 Slim?** Time your purchase to a Shopee/Lazada platform event (3.3, 4.4, 5.5, 6.6, 7.7, 8.8, 9.9, 10.10, 11.11, 12.12). Stack platform vouchers, free-shipping coins, and credit-card rebates.
- **Want 24-month 0% instalment?** Challenger, Courts, and Harvey Norman all run 0% instalment plans with major SG banks (DBS, UOB, OCBC, HSBC, Citi). The headline price is official retail.
- **Trading in old PS4/PS5 games?** Sony Centre SG, GameChan, Qisahn, and Stereo all run trade-in programs. Bundle with a new console and you can offset SGD 50–300 of the new device price.
- **Buying PS5 Pro?** PS5 Pro is mainly available at Sony Centre SG, authorised game retailers, and a small number of Shopee/Lazada sellers. Supply is controlled — expect official retail pricing throughout launch.

## Common questions

**Are PS5 prices the same across all Singapore merchants?** The official retail price is identical at Sony Centre SG, Challenger, Courts, Harvey Norman, and Best Denki. Shopee Mall and LazMall can undercut by SGD 30–80 during platform sales for PS5 Slim. PS5 Pro is rarely discounted.

**PS5 Slim Disc vs Digital — which is the better value in Singapore?** PS5 Slim Disc is SGD 100 above PS5 Slim Digital. PS5 Slim Digital is the budget pick if you're all-digital.

**Where do I buy PS5 Pro in Singapore?** PS5 Pro is available at Sony Centre SG, selected authorised game retailers, and a small number of Shopee/Lazada sellers. For trade-in and bundle deals, Sony Centre SG is the most reliable source.
