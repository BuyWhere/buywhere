## CAAS registration — read before flying

All Singapore drone operators must follow [CAAS rules](https://www.caas.gov.sg/operations/drones). The Mini 4 Pro is **249g with the standard battery** (under the 250g threshold) but **crosses 250g with the Intelligent Flight Battery Plus**. CAAS classifies drones by weight:

- **Class 1 (≤1.5 kg)**: SGD 20/year registration, no Activity Permit needed for recreational flying
- **Class 2 (>1.5 kg)**: SGD 60/year registration plus Activity Permit for commercial work

You must also follow CAAS operating rules — no flying over crowds, no flying near airports, no flying above 400 ft AGL. Read the CAAS drone portal before your first flight.

## Mini 4 Pro vs alternatives

- **Mini 4 Pro vs Mini 3 Pro**: Mini 4 Pro adds omnidirectional obstacle sensing, 4K/100fps slow-mo, and HDR video. Worth the SGD 250 premium for most buyers.
- **Mini 4 Pro vs Air 3**: Air 3 is larger, has a 1/1.3" main + 1/1.3" tele camera, but crosses 250g and requires full Class 1 registration. Better for serious videographers.
- **Mini 4 Pro vs Mavic 3 Pro**: Mavic 3 Pro is the pro pick (Hasselblad camera, 4/3 sensor) but 5× the price and >900g.

## Common questions

**Can I fly the Mini 4 Pro anywhere in Singapore?** No. CAAS designates no-fly zones (Changi Airport, military bases, government buildings, some nature reserves). Check the [CAAS FlySG zone map](https://flysg.lta.gov.sg) before flying.

**Is the DJI RC 2 worth the upgrade?** Yes if you have a recent iPhone / Android you don't want to mount. The RC 2 has a 5.5" built-in screen, doesn't drain your phone, and starts faster.

**What if my DJI Mini 4 Pro crashes?** DJI Care Refresh (SGD 119/year) covers 2 replacements per year at reduced cost. Buy within 48 hours of activating the drone.
