### Important caveat on this metric's design

**The sweep's `deliver_to_pass_rate` is the probe-side metric**, not the
adoption-side metric. Because the sweep itself constructs the request body and
always sets `deliver_to` on v2 calls, once Rex lands BUY-72533 the sweep's
`by_mode.v2.deliver_to_pass_rate` will be 1.0 by construction (gate accepts
calls that include deliver_to; sweep always includes deliver_to).

The **real adoption metric** — of all v2 calls reaching the server in the
rolling 24h window from real agents, what fraction had `deliver_to` in the
request body — requires **server-side log aggregation**. That is a Pulse/Atlas
deliverable, not Cart's. The sweep is the **measurement-vehicle scaffold**
that:
- Confirms the v2 wire is live (`v2_tools_live == 5` after Rex ships)
- Confirms the gate fires correctly (rpc -32602 with "deliver_to is REQUIRED"
  when called without deliver_to — Atlas's responsibility, but our sweep's
  negative-control subset exercises this)
- Emits the CSV that BUY-72535 contract item #4 requires

Once the wire is live, daily CSV + JSONL goes to this issue as work-products
per the contract. Real adoption numbers come from a follow-up wiring that
queries `monitoring.sweep_results` (or a Pulse-access-log query) and rolls
them up to `by_mode.v2.deliver_to_pass_rate`.

### api_error spike note

The 72 v2 + 104 v1 api_error cells in the first run (32% / 46%) is high
relative to a steady-state p13 sweep (~5-10% error rate is normal). Likely
causes:
- The sweep adds load on top of existing fetch-probe / hue-probe / p13 sweeps
  running in the same 23:55Z-14:30Z window.
- 225 cells × ~600ms each = ~140s of sustained MCP traffic.

Recommend lowering `SWEEP_CONCURRENCY=1` (already default) and bumping
`SWEEP_MIN_DELAY_MS=500` for daily cron once the surface is live, to stay
under the 200-rpm monitoring tier (BUY-71309 precedent).
