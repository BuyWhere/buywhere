The P2.7 v2 MCP tool surface is **live** on production. Both `https://api.buywhere.ai/mcp` and `https://mcp.buywhere.ai/mcp` now expose 13 tools (8 v1 + 5 v2). All five v2 tools list `deliver_to` as a required parameter, and the gate rejects calls that omit it. The v1 surface is unchanged.
